Quick verdict

Choose Scrambly if you mainly want game and app offers and a fast first cash-out: PayPal from $1, paid within minutes once you pass a one-time ID and selfie check. Choose Swagbucks if you want variety and a long track record: surveys, games, receipts and shopping cash back, with gift cards from $1 and PayPal from $5. Many people use both, because the same game offer can pay differently on each.

Short answer: Pick Scrambly if you mostly want to earn from mobile games and want your first payout fast: PayPal from $1, and Scrambly says money arrives within minutes once you pass a one-time ID and selfie check. Pick Swagbucks if you want more ways to earn (surveys, receipts, shopping cash back as well as games) and a company with a long track record; PayPal starts at $5 and usually arrives within a few days.

Both are free to join and both pay real money. This comparison is for US users and uses each company’s own help pages and store listings, checked November 22, 2026. For the full picture on each, read our Scrambly review and Swagbucks review.

The key differences

1. What you’ll actually do. Scrambly is built around sponsored game and app offers: install a game, hit milestones, get coins. It adds surveys and small daily walking rewards. Swagbucks is much broader: surveys, games, grocery receipts, shopping cash back and offers, on the web as well as in the app. If you don’t enjoy mobile games, Swagbucks has far more for you to do.

2. How low and how fast you can cash out. Scrambly’s standard minimum is 1,000 coins ($1) for PayPal, Visa, Cash App and some gift cards (bank transfer needs 10,000 coins). Swagbucks’ PayPal minimum is $5, which costs 530 SB because PayPal rewards carry a 30 SB fee; some gift cards start at $1. On speed, Scrambly says rewards arrive within minutes after verification, while Swagbucks says PayPal usually takes a couple of days and allows up to 10 business days.

3. Verification. Scrambly asks for a government ID and a live selfie once, before your first withdrawal. Swagbucks doesn’t describe a photo-ID step for PayPal, but your name and email must match your PayPal account and PayPal must show a confirmed bank.

4. Track record. Swagbucks is run by Prodege, LLC, which has operated rewards programs since 2008 and is BBB-accredited with an A- rating. Scrambly (SCRAMBLY SRL) is a much younger company. That doesn’t make Scrambly unsafe, but Swagbucks has a longer public history.

Bonuses: smaller but simpler vs bigger with strings

  • Scrambly gives new users 500 welcome coins (about $0.50). Small, but there’s nothing to buy.
  • Swagbucks advertises a $10 bonus, but you only get it after activating it and spending at least $25 at a Swagbucks Shop store within 30 days of signing up.

If you were going to shop online anyway, the Swagbucks bonus is worth more. If not, ignore it. Never buy something just to unlock a bonus.

Game offers: why using both can pay

Both apps list offers for many of the same mobile games, but the rewards and milestones can differ. One r/SwagBucks user who uses both said Scrambly’s offers are “generally a bit lower than Swagbucks” but that it runs periodic bonuses and has games they can’t find on Swagbucks. Because most game offers only credit your first install, the smart move is to compare the offer on both apps before installing, then start it from the one that pays more.

Tips for either app:

  1. Install fresh from inside the app, on the device you signed up with. No VPN.
  2. Screenshot the offer terms and every milestone you reach.
  3. Watch deadlines. Many milestones expire after a set number of days.
  4. Skip offers that need spending unless you’d spend that money anyway.

Complaints on both sides

Neither app is complaint-free. For Swagbucks, BBB lists about 2,600 complaints in three years against Prodege (across all its brands), often about offers that didn’t track or accounts held for review at cash-out. For Scrambly, users praise fast payouts but report slow support and occasional account freezes during withdrawal checks; one long-time user on r/SwagBucks said support “takes on average at least a week” to answer a ticket. Our advice is the same for both: use your real details, keep screenshots and cash out regularly.

Which one should you start with?

  • Just want to see a rewards app pay you? Start with Scrambly: earn $1, verify once, cash out to PayPal. Our Scrambly link works on iPhone; Android users can find Scrambly on Google Play.
  • Want a long-term, all-round earner? Start with Swagbucks and use cash back and receipts for things you’d buy anyway.
  • Into mobile games? Use both and compare each game offer before you install.

The bottom line

Scrambly wins on speed and a low minimum; Swagbucks wins on variety and track record. Both are legit, both are free, and both work best as a small extra rather than income. If you only install one, let the kind of tasks you actually enjoy decide.

Frequently asked questions

Is Scrambly or Swagbucks better?

Neither is better for everyone. Scrambly is better for a fast, low first cash-out ($1 to PayPal, within minutes after verification) and for game offers. Swagbucks is better for variety (surveys, receipts, shopping cash back) and has a much longer track record.

Which pays faster, Scrambly or Swagbucks?

Scrambly says rewards arrive within minutes once your account is verified. Swagbucks says PayPal deposits usually arrive within a couple of days, and its PayPal reward page allows up to 10 business days.

What are the minimum cash-outs?

Scrambly's standard minimum is 1,000 coins ($1) for PayPal and several other options; bank transfer needs 10,000 coins. Swagbucks' PayPal minimum is $5, which costs 530 SB, and some gift cards start at $1.

Can I use Scrambly and Swagbucks at the same time?

Yes. They're separate companies and nothing stops you from having an account on each. Just don't do the same game offer on both: offers usually only credit for a first install, so pick the app that pays more for that game before you start.

Do Scrambly and Swagbucks require ID?

Scrambly requires a one-time government ID and live selfie before your first withdrawal. Swagbucks doesn't describe an ID-photo step for PayPal, but your names and email must match between Swagbucks and PayPal and PayPal must show a confirmed bank.

Are Scrambly and Swagbucks legit?

Both are real apps that pay. Swagbucks is run by Prodege, LLC (BBB-accredited, A-), and Scrambly by SCRAMBLY SRL. Both have complaints about offers not tracking and accounts held at cash-out, so screenshot your progress and cash out regularly.
