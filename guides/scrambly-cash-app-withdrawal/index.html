How to withdraw from Scrambly to Cash App

  1. Verify your account (first time only). Scrambly asks for photos of both sides of a US government ID (driver’s license, ID card, passport or residence permit) and a real-time selfie. It says this takes a few minutes.
  2. Reach 1,000 coins. That’s $1. Coins come from game and app milestones, the 500-coin welcome bonus and small walking rewards.
  3. Open the Withdraw tab and choose Cash App under Cash.
  4. Pick an amount and confirm. Double-check you chose the right method: once finalized, it can’t be changed.
  5. Redeem the reward link. Payouts are sent through Tremendous. Open the reward (email from rewards@reward.tremendous.com, a text, or the link Scrambly shows), choose Cash App, then approve the transfer in the Cash App app.
  6. Check Cash App activity. Tremendous says the transfer then shows up in your Cash App activity.

How long does it take?

There are two parts: Scrambly releasing the payout, and the transfer reaching Cash App.

  • Scrambly’s side: its help center says payouts usually appear within a couple of minutes, with occasional delays up to 24 hours. If you see “Your reward needs to be reviewed by sender,” support reviews it manually, usually within 24 hours.
  • Tremendous’ side: Tremendous says Cash App transfers usually arrive within minutes once you approve them in Cash App.
  • What users report: in a May 2026 r/Scrambly_Official thread, users said Cash App payouts took one to three days, felt like a direct deposit, and that cash-outs on Thursday or later sometimes didn’t arrive until the next week. Others said theirs arrived within a day. In summer 2026, several users also said more withdrawals were going to review.

Our take: expect Cash App to work, but don’t count on minutes. If you need money today, use PayPal, which has the same $1 minimum. You can move it from PayPal to Cash App yourself afterward.

Cash App withdrawal stuck? Fixes that work

Scrambly’s help center lists the most common cases:

  • A “Redeem” button is still showing in Profile → History → Withdrawals. The withdrawal wasn’t finished. Tap Redeem to complete it; the coins were deducted but not sent yet.
  • No Redeem button, but no money yet. The withdrawal went through. Wait up to 24 hours, and check your email and texts for the Tremendous reward link; you may still need to approve it in Cash App.
  • “Your reward needs to be reviewed by sender.” Manual review by Scrambly support, usually under 24 hours. No ticket needed.
  • “Oops, something went wrong.” The withdrawal didn’t go through. Report it through Scrambly’s outage form.
  • “Balance double-verification is required.” A security check. Contact support under that category.
  • Wrong method chosen. If it still shows Redeem, contact support before finalizing; they can cancel it and refund your coins.

Take a screenshot of each withdrawal. If something goes wrong, it makes support much faster.

Watch out for “Scrambly Cash App” scams

Search “Scrambly Cash App” and you’ll find posts offering Cash App money for your verified Scrambly account. One April 2026 post on r/CashApp asked users to paste their one-time login link, which gives the poster full control of your account. Commenters immediately flagged it as a scam.

Rules to stay safe:

  • Never share a login link, code or your ID photos with anyone.
  • Only cash out inside the official app or scrambly.io. Real rewards come from Tremendous emails or texts, not DMs.
  • Ignore “guaranteed” amounts. Scrambly pays for completing offers; nobody can promise a fixed payout for signing up.

Is Cash App the best way to cash out from Scrambly?

It’s a good option if Cash App is where you keep your money and you’re OK waiting a bit. For the fastest results, PayPal wins at the same $1 minimum. For a small bonus, Visa (+2%) is worth a look.

Frequently asked questions

Does Scrambly pay to Cash App?

Yes. Scrambly's website lists Cash App as a withdrawal option with a $1 minimum. You need a verified Scrambly account (ID photos and a live selfie) and a Cash App account to receive it.

How long does a Scrambly Cash App withdrawal take?

Scrambly says most payouts appear within a couple of minutes, with occasional delays up to 24 hours. Tremendous, which delivers the payout, says Cash App transfers usually arrive within minutes. Some Scrambly users on Reddit in 2026 reported Cash App payouts taking one to three days, especially late in the week.

What is the Scrambly minimum for Cash App?

1,000 coins, which Scrambly shows as $1. That's the same minimum as PayPal and Visa. Bank transfer needs $10 and some gift cards need $5.

Why is my Scrambly Cash App withdrawal pending?

Check your Scrambly withdrawal history. If a 'Redeem' button is still showing, the withdrawal isn't finished; tap it to complete it. If you see 'Your reward needs to be reviewed by sender', Scrambly's support reviews it manually, usually within 24 hours. Also check your email and texts for the Tremendous reward link.

Can I change a Scrambly withdrawal from Cash App to PayPal?

Only before it's finalized. Scrambly's help center says support can cancel an unfinished withdrawal (one still showing 'Redeem') and refund the coins. Once it's finalized, support can't change the payment method.

Is it safe to sell or share a Scrambly account for Cash App money?

No. Posts offering to 'buy' verified Scrambly accounts for Cash App payments ask for your one-time login link, which hands over your account and ID-verified balance. Never share login links or codes.
