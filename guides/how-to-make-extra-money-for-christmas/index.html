1. Sell things you already own (fastest)

This is the one method that costs nothing, needs nobody’s approval and can pay within days. Electronics, tools, small appliances, kids’ gear, sports equipment and furniture tend to move, and gift-able items in good condition can sell well in December.

  • Price it to sell: search recent sold listings, not asking prices, and check the platform’s current seller fees.
  • Mind the calendar: for delivery before December 25 in the lower 48, USPS recommends shipping Ground Advantage and First-Class Mail by Dec. 17 and Priority Mail by Dec. 18, 2026. Those are recommended dates, not guarantees.
  • Stay safe: meet local buyers in a public place, and never accept an overpayment and refund the difference; that is a classic scam.

2. Offer holiday help in your neighborhood

People will pay to get time back in December. Ideas that need little or no equipment:

  • Hanging and taking down holiday lights (ground-level jobs if you avoid ladders)
  • Gift wrapping and assembling toys or furniture
  • Pet sitting and house sitting while families travel
  • Errands, holiday party cleanup, snow shoveling or yard cleanup

Post a simple flat-price offer in neighborhood groups, and agree on the job, the price and who covers any damage before you start.

3. Pick up a seasonal job

Retailers, warehouses, shipping companies and restaurants add staff for the rush, and some roles last into January. Apply directly on the company’s own careers page, and ask about shift flexibility and employee discounts. The FTC warns that real employers do not ask you to pay for a job, training or equipment, and do not send you a check to deposit and partly send back.

4. Drive or shop for a delivery app

Food and grocery delivery apps let you work evenings and weekends, and some offer faster cash-out options for a fee. Approval and background checks can take days, so apply early. Gas and wear on your car come out of what you earn, so keep a mileage log; you will want it at tax time.

5. Free rewards apps for spare minutes

Rewards apps pay per completed survey or game milestone, so think of them as extra money while you wait in line or watch TV, not as the main Christmas plan. Start now so a first cash-out has time to clear.

6. Make and sell holiday items

Ornaments, wreaths, candles, printable cards and personalized gifts sell in the weeks before Christmas, online and at local craft fairs. Build in time for making and shipping, and if you sell food, check your state and local rules first. A brand-new online shop can take weeks to get noticed, so treat this as a bonus.

What to avoid this season

  • Jobs that charge you for training, a starter kit or equipment, or that send a check to deposit and partly return. The FTC lists these as common job scams.
  • “Earn $500 before Christmas” ads for tapping a phone. Legitimate apps show a per-task reward and a cash-out minimum; they do not guarantee a daily amount.
  • Expensive short-term borrowing for gifts. Cash advances and similar products can cost far more than they seem once fees are counted. A smaller gift list costs less.
  • Apps with heavy payout complaints. Benjamin has had withdrawals in a processing queue since mid-2026, and Playful Rewards has a BBB F rating; read our reviews before relying on either before Christmas.

Report suspicious offers at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest way to make money for Christmas?

Selling things you already own is usually the fastest, because local buyers can pay the same day. Local holiday help such as hanging lights, wrapping gifts or pet sitting can also pay within days. Free rewards apps add smaller amounts: Scrambly lets you cash out from $1 after a one-time ID check.

How can I make extra money for Christmas online?

Sell items on online marketplaces, offer a skill you already have (design, writing, tutoring) on freelance sites, and use free rewards apps such as Swagbucks, Scrambly or Survey Club in spare minutes. Online selling still needs shipping time: USPS recommends Dec. 17 for Ground Advantage for delivery before Dec. 25 in the lower 48.

How much money can I make before Christmas?

It depends on what you have to sell, how many hours you can work, and where you live, so any single number would be a guess. Selling items and taking shifts usually adds up faster than rewards apps, which pay per completed survey or game milestone.

Is it too late to get a seasonal job for Christmas?

Not always. Many retailers, warehouses and delivery services keep hiring as holiday shifts open up. Apply directly on the company's own careers page, and avoid any 'job' that asks you to pay for training, equipment or a background check up front.

Can I make Christmas money from my phone without paying anything?

Yes. Selling apps, free rewards apps and cash back on purchases you were already making all work without a sign-up fee. Some game offers include optional in-app purchases you can skip. Never pay a deposit to unlock earnings or a withdrawal.

Should I take out a cash advance for Christmas gifts?

Be careful. Short-term cash advances and similar products can carry very high costs once fees are counted. Try selling items, picking up shifts or trimming your gift list first, and read the full cost before you borrow.
