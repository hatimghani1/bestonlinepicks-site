Short answer: You can get gift cards without paying for them by redeeming points: points from free rewards apps (surveys, game offers, receipts), and points you already earn from store loyalty programs, credit cards or Microsoft Rewards. Nothing legitimate asks you to pay a fee, enter your card number or buy something first to “unlock” a free card.

Among the free apps we cover, start with Freecash for Amazon and other gift cards (no official cash-out fee on gift cards). Swagbucks has the widest gift card store, Scrambly has the lowest minimums (from $1 on several options), and Survey Club is the survey-first pick on iPhone. What “without paying” really costs you

A gift card from a rewards program is free in dollars, not in everything else:

  • Time: surveys, game milestones and daily tasks take minutes to hours per card.
  • Data: survey and receipt apps are paid by market research and advertising. Your answers and shopping habits are part of the deal.
  • Verification: many apps run a one-time ID, phone or account check before your first redemption.
  • Optional spending: some game offers include in-app purchases, and cash back only pays when you shop. You can skip both if you want to spend $0.

1. Check the points you already have

Before you download anything, look for value you may already own: grocery or pharmacy loyalty points, airline or hotel points, and credit card rewards. Many programs let you redeem for gift cards. Compare the redemption rate with a statement credit or cash back first, because gift cards are sometimes the weaker option.

Microsoft Rewards is a free, official program that awards points for searches with Bing and daily tasks, redeemable for gift cards. Check its current catalog and rules on Microsoft’s site.

3. Swagbucks: the biggest gift card store

Swagbucks (Prodege, LLC) pays points called SB, at 100 SB = $1, for surveys, game offers, receipt scanning and shopping cash back. Its rewards store lists cards from big retailers such as Amazon, Walmart and Target, many from $5 (500 SB) when we checked, and some cards are listed at lower amounts. Catalog and prices change, so check the store before you aim for one brand.

Speed: Swagbucks’ help center says electronic gift cards take up to 5 business days. If you would rather have cash, PayPal starts at $5 for 530 SB (30 SB covers PayPal’s fee).

The catch: surveys often screen you out partway through, and the $10 welcome bonus only counts after a $25+ Swagbucks Shop purchase within 30 days. If surveys stop loading, see Swagbucks surveys not working.

7. Cash back and receipts on shopping you already do

If you are buying groceries or holiday gifts anyway, receipt scanning and shopping cash back can turn that spending into points you redeem for gift cards. Swagbucks combines both with its surveys and games. The rule is simple: never buy something just to earn the rebate. Cash back usually shows as pending for a while before you can redeem it, so check each store’s terms.

Other apps also cover this, including Benjamin, which mixes cash back, games and surveys. Read our review first: since mid-2026 its withdrawals go into a processing queue, and many users report long waits.

8. Giveaways: fine as a bonus, never as a plan

Brands and retailers run real sweepstakes, usually announced on their own verified accounts or websites with official rules. A real giveaway never asks you to pay, share a card number or “verify” with a code sent to your phone. Treat any win as a bonus.

Free gift card scams to avoid

The FTC warns that gift cards are a favorite payment method for scammers, because they are hard to trace. Walk away from:

  • Pop-ups, texts or emails saying you won a $500 or $1,000 gift card and only need to enter card details or pay “shipping.”
  • “Gift card generators” or codes from YouTube and TikTok comments. They do not work and often lead to malware or survey loops.
  • Anyone who asks you to buy gift cards and share the numbers, or to pay for a job, prize or fee with them.
  • Offers to buy or “verify” your rewards account.

Report scams at ReportFraud.ftc.gov. If you paid a scammer with a gift card, the FTC advises contacting the company that issued the card right away.

Frequently asked questions

Can you really get free gift cards without paying?

Yes, as long as 'free' means you never hand over money. Rewards apps such as Swagbucks and Scrambly pay points for surveys and game offers, which you redeem for gift cards. Loyalty and credit card points you already earn can often be redeemed for gift cards too. You still spend time, share some data, and usually pass a one-time account or ID check before the first redemption.

What is the fastest way to get a free gift card?

Among the apps we cover, Scrambly has the lowest minimums (from $1 on several options, $5 for some brands such as Amazon) and says rewards arrive within minutes after a one-time ID and selfie check. Points you already have in a loyalty or credit card program can be even faster, because you skip the earning step.

How do I get a free Amazon gift card?

Freecash, Swagbucks, Scrambly, Survey Club and ZipRewards all list Amazon among their gift card choices, with different minimums: Freecash typically starts gift cards at $5 after your first (region-dependent) cash-out, Swagbucks lists many cards from $5 (500 SB), Scrambly lists Amazon at $5, Survey Club needs $20 on the free plan, and ZipRewards needs $25. Catalogs change, so check the redemption page before you start. Avoid sites that promise an Amazon card in exchange for your card number or a 'shipping fee'.

Are free gift card websites a scam?

Many are. Real rewards programs show what each task pays and a cash-out minimum up front, and they never ask you to pay. Pop-ups, texts and social posts that say you 'won' a large gift card and only need to enter card details, pay a fee or install something are scams. Report them at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Do I have to pay taxes on free gift cards?

Possibly. Gift cards earned from surveys, games and similar activities can be taxable income. Swagbucks' tax help page, for example, says that once your gift card and other qualifying redemptions reach $2,000 in a calendar year, you must submit tax information to keep redeeming (PayPal and Gambit excepted). Ask a tax professional about your situation.

Can teenagers get free gift cards this way?

This guide is written for US adults. ZipRewards requires users to be 18 or older, and other apps and payout services set their own age rules. Always read the current terms before signing up.
