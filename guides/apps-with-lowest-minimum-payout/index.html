How we checked these numbers (methodology)

  • Sources: each app’s official website, help center, terms of service or US App Store / Google Play listing, read in November 2026. All of them are listed under “Sources we checked” at the end of the page.
  • Minimum cashout means the smallest withdrawal the app lists for a US account. Where the minimum differs by method, we show the cheapest one and the PayPal one.
  • Varies means the company does not publish one figure (it sets it per account, per country, or only inside the app), or we could not confirm it on an official page.
  • Conflicts: when two official pages disagree (InboxDollars), we show both.
  • Not a ranking of earnings. Minimums say nothing about how fast you will reach them. We do not publish hourly rates.

Apps that pay instantly to PayPal

“Instant” usually means after approval. Almost every app holds the first cash-out for an email, phone or ID check. After that, these apps describe PayPal payouts as instant or within minutes:

  1. Freecash: PayPal is typically instant once approved, from $5 after your first cash-out. The first withdrawal needs email verification and a one-time ID check.
  2. Cash Giraffe: PayPal listed as instant, from $0.50 in the US.
  3. JustPlay: cash out every 3 hours; the money “should be visible on your PayPal balance within just a moment.”
  4. Scrambly: within minutes from $1, after a one-time ID and selfie check. Some payouts are reviewed, usually in under 24 hours.
  5. AttaPoll: says most PayPal payments arrive within minutes. The minimum depends on your country.

To get your first PayPal cash-out through faster:

  • Use the same email on the app and on PayPal (Google Opinion Rewards, InboxDollars and Swagbucks all require or recommend this).
  • Make sure your PayPal account is confirmed with a bank or card. Several apps reject unconfirmed accounts.
  • Finish the ID check as soon as the app asks. It is a one-time step.
  • PayPal is generally 18+ in the US. If you are 16 or 17, cash out to gift cards instead. Freecash allows 16+ for gift cards.

Frequently asked questions

Which app has the lowest minimum payout?

In our November 2026 check, Cash Giraffe lists a $0.50 PayPal minimum for US users, and JustPlay's terms say there is usually no minimum threshold to redeem rewards. Scrambly, PenguPace and Playful Rewards list $1. Google Opinion Rewards sends $2 to PayPal automatically on iPhone.

Which apps pay instantly to PayPal?

Freecash says PayPal cash-outs are typically instant once approved. Cash Giraffe and Scrambly describe PayPal payouts as instant or within minutes, JustPlay says money should show in PayPal within a moment, and AttaPoll says most payments arrive within minutes. First cash-outs usually need an ID or account check first, so the first one can take longer.

What apps pay a $1 minimum?

Scrambly ($1 PayPal and several gift cards), PenguPace ($1, Android) and Playful Rewards ($1 per its FAQ) list a $1 minimum. Swagbucks has some gift cards from $1, though PayPal starts at $5. Cash Giraffe goes lower, with a $0.50 PayPal minimum in the US.

What is the minimum payout on Freecash?

Freecash's first cash-out minimum depends on your region and is shown on your Cashout page; its FAQ describes $5 to $20 for the first withdrawal. After the first cash-out, PayPal and most gift cards typically start at $5. PayPal and some other cash methods carry a fee, while gift cards have none.

Why do some apps say 'varies' in this table?

We only print a number when the app's own site, help center, terms or store listing states it. Some apps set the minimum per account or per country and show it only inside the app, so we write 'varies' instead of guessing.
