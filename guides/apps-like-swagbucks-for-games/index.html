How Swagbucks game offers work

Swagbucks pays in points called SB, and 100 SB = $1. For games, there are two routes:

  1. Partner game offers (in the Discover section). You install a mobile game through Swagbucks’ link and earn SB when you reach a goal, such as a level or a building. Rewards and deadlines vary by offer, device and country, so read the offer’s details before installing.
  2. Swagbucks’ own games, which run inside Swagbucks and pay small amounts.

Swagbucks also says you can earn SB for in-game purchases with some gaming partners. As with every app here, we don’t recommend spending money to earn rewards.

Tracking matters. Swagbucks credits a game offer only when your first click appears in your Ledger. A VPN, an ad blocker, a previous install of the game, or downloading it from the store before clicking the offer can all block credit.

Cashing out from Swagbucks

  • PayPal starts at $5, which costs 530 SB because Swagbucks adds a 30 SB fee to each PayPal reward (it says this covers PayPal’s fees). Its store lists PayPal from $5 to $100.
  • Gift cards can start lower, and Swagbucks occasionally offers discounted gift cards.
  • First PayPal reward: the names on Swagbucks and PayPal must match exactly, your Swagbucks email must be added to your PayPal account, and PayPal must show a confirmed bank.
  • Timing: Swagbucks’ help center says electronic gift cards and PayPal transfers each take up to 5 business days.

The welcome bonus fine print

Swagbucks advertises a $10 bonus (1,000 SB), but per its own terms you have to activate it in the Swag Ups section and spend at least $25 at a store in Swagbucks Shop (earning at least 25 SB) within 30 days of registering. It’s not free money for playing games.

Downsides to know

Independent reviewers (such as Finder) report recurring complaints on BBB and Trustpilot about accounts being suspended around redemption time, and note that Swagbucks’ app store listings disclose extensive data sharing and tracking. Read the privacy details and cash out regularly.

4. Playback: Android players

Playback is an Android-only app with a $5 minimum and many payout options (PayPal, Venmo, Visa, Amazon, bank transfer and more). It needs usage-access permission and also offers cash back on eligible in-game purchases.

4. Reward Rush: games plus surveys

Reward Rush mixes game milestones, surveys and app offers with PayPal and gift card payouts. Its minimum and withdrawal goals are set per account on the cash-out screen, and points expire after 12 months.

Best for: people who want a Swagbucks-style mix in a lighter app.

How to choose

  • Want the fastest proof an app pays? Start with Scrambly’s $1 minimum.
  • Like surveys and shopping too? Swagbucks covers far more than games.

Frequently asked questions

How do you earn on Swagbucks by playing games?

Mostly through partner game offers in Swagbucks' Discover section: you install a game through Swagbucks' link and earn SB when you reach a goal such as a level. Swagbucks also has a few of its own simple games that pay small amounts. Some partner offers involve in-game purchases, so read the offer details first.

What is the Swagbucks minimum cash-out?

Swagbucks' rewards store lists PayPal from $5 for 530 SB, which includes a 30 SB PayPal fee. Gift cards can start lower. Every 100 SB is worth $1.

What app is most like Swagbucks but for games?

Freecash is the closest all-round alternative: game offers, surveys and PayPal or gift-card cash-outs, for users 16+. Scrambly is the lowest-minimum game-focused option ($1 to PayPal and some cards). ZipRewards shows dollar amounts per milestone but needs $25. Playback suits Android players with a $5 minimum.

Why didn't my Swagbucks game offer credit?

Common reasons include using a VPN or ad blocker, having installed the game before, or downloading it from the app store before clicking the offer. Swagbucks credits game offers only when your first click shows in your Ledger, so check it after you click and after each goal.

Does Swagbucks require verification to cash out?

For your first PayPal reward, Swagbucks' help center says your first and last names must match on both accounts, your Swagbucks email must be on your PayPal account, and PayPal must show a confirmed bank. Orders can also require a one-time PIN check.

Can I use these apps outside the US?

This guide is written for US readers. Swagbucks and Scrambly support some other countries, while ZipRewards is US-only. Check each app's eligibility rules.
