Short answer: Yes, Scrambly is legit, and it’s one of the best “first app” choices for US users who want to check that a rewards app really pays. You can withdraw from 1,000 coins ($1) to PayPal and several other options, and Scrambly says payouts arrive within minutes. Scrambly is available in the US, UK and Canada. This review focuses on US users. Note: our affiliate link for Scrambly currently works for iPhone; Android users can find Scrambly on Google Play.

What Scrambly is

Scrambly is a rewards app from SCRAMBLY SRL (the seller name on the App Store), which independent reviewers identify as based in Bologna, Italy, and which also makes the PenguPace walking app. On its website Scrambly describes its apps as a way to discover games and apps, stay active and earn rewards.

How it works, per Scrambly’s own steps:

  1. Sign up and receive a 500-coin welcome bonus.
  2. Choose a game or app on the Discovery page and tap “Get Started.”
  3. Reach milestones. Every milestone earns coins, starting from early levels.
  4. Verify your identity once (about 3-4 minutes, according to Scrambly).
  5. Withdraw from 1,000 coins.

The coin rate is simple: 1,000 coins = $1. Scrambly also offers cash back on in-app purchases in apps you discover through it, plus promo codes on its social accounts and occasional bonuses. As always, we don’t recommend spending money in games to earn rewards.

Cashing out: the $1 minimum

Scrambly’s help center says the minimum withdrawal is 1,000 coins, and that some methods need more. Its website lists $1 minimums for options including PayPal, Visa and Cash App, with other options at $5, $10 or $25, and charity donations (such as the American Red Cross) from $5. Bank transfer requires at least 10,000 coins (about $10).

Two useful details:

  • Any PayPal address works. Scrambly doesn’t link your PayPal to your account, so you enter the address during withdrawal.
  • Speed. Scrambly says rewards arrive “in minutes.” Plenty of users agree in reviews, but treat that as typical, not guaranteed.

Why the $1 floor matters: you can test the entire process, sign-up to verification to cash in your PayPal, with the welcome bonus plus one quick offer. Compare that with $25 at ZipRewards or $50 at Reco Social.

Identity verification: what you’ll need

Before your first withdrawal, Scrambly requires a one-time identity check. From its help center:

  • You can start it from the onboarding section in your Profile or during your first withdrawal.
  • You take photos of both sides of your document and a real-time selfie.
  • Accepted documents: ID card, driver’s license, passport or residence permit.
  • Verification is available only with government-issued IDs from the US, UK or Canada.
  • It must be done by the account owner; using someone else’s documents is prohibited.

Scrambly explains that verification protects accounts and lets advertisers offer higher payouts because they trust the audience. That’s a fair trade for most people, but if you’re not comfortable sharing ID with a rewards app, Scrambly isn’t for you.

Walking rewards

Scrambly pays a small amount for steps, tracked by your phone. Since April 2026, its help center says:

  • The daily walking cap doubled to 160 coins (about 16 cents).
  • To keep redeeming walking rewards, you must complete at least one offer step every 2 days.

Walking is a bonus, not an income stream. For the full math on step apps, see our guide to walking apps that pay real money.

The fine print on offers

Offers are where Scrambly earnings come from, and offers are where most complaints come from. Offerwall terms like Scrambly’s commonly say a reward is only earned once the activity is tracked, attributed and validated, and that progress shown in the app doesn’t guarantee a reward. Independent reviewers note that changing device, resetting your advertising ID, reinstalling the game or using a VPN can make an offer ineligible.

To protect yourself:

  • Start every offer from inside Scrambly.
  • Pick games you’ve never installed on that device.
  • Stay on one device for the whole offer, and don’t reinstall.
  • No VPN, no ad-ID resets.
  • Watch the deadline on each offer.
  • Screenshot milestones. If something doesn’t credit, contact support early, well before the offer expires.

Is Scrambly legit? Weighing the evidence

In favor:

  • A named company with apps on both stores in the US.
  • A help center that spells out minimums, verification and walking rules, including unpopular changes.
  • A $1 minimum that lets you verify payouts yourself almost immediately.
  • Scrambly displays its Trustpilot reviews on its homepage. Read the recent negative ones on Trustpilot itself too, as you should for any app.

Against:

  • ID + selfie before you can withdraw anything.
  • Offer tracking isn’t perfect, and support can take time on missing-credit tickets.
  • Walking rewards have strings attached.

Our verdict: Scrambly is legit and a sensible first rewards app for US users, especially because you can test the payout for $1.

Who Scrambly is for

Great fit: US gamers who want a low minimum and fast payouts, iPhone or Android users, and people who don’t mind a one-time ID check.

Not a fit: anyone unwilling to share ID, people who only want to be paid for walking, or anyone hoping to replace a paycheck. Like every app here, Scrambly is extra cash, not income.

The bottom line

Scrambly earns its reputation: a $1 minimum, withdrawals to any PayPal address, and payouts it says land in minutes once you’ve passed a one-time ID and selfie check. Use it for offers on games you’d enjoy anyway, follow the tracking tips above, and cash out often.

Frequently asked questions

Is Scrambly legit?

Yes. Scrambly is run by SCRAMBLY SRL, is on the US App Store and Google Play, publishes a help center with clear withdrawal and verification rules, and supports PayPal withdrawals from $1. As with any offer app, tracking problems can happen.

What is the Scrambly minimum withdrawal?

The standard minimum is 1,000 coins, which Scrambly advertises as $1, for options such as PayPal, Visa and Cash App. Some methods need more; bank transfer requires at least 10,000 coins.

Does Scrambly require ID verification?

Yes, once, before your first withdrawal. You photograph both sides of a government ID (ID card, driver's license, passport or residence permit) from the US, UK or Canada and take a real-time selfie. Scrambly says it takes a few minutes.

How fast does Scrambly pay?

Scrambly says rewards arrive in your account within minutes once your account is verified. Individual experiences can vary.

Can I withdraw Scrambly earnings to any PayPal account?

Scrambly's help center says you can withdraw to any PayPal address; it isn't linked to your Scrambly account. You enter the address during withdrawal.

Does Scrambly pay for walking?

Yes, a little. Walking rewards can reach up to 160 coins (about 16 cents) per day since April 2026, and they stay redeemable only if you complete at least one offer step every two days.
