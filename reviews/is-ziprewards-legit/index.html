Short answer: ZipRewards looks like a legitimate US rewards app, with a big asterisk on speed. It pays real money for reaching milestones in mobile games you haven’t played before, but you need $25 to cash out, you must verify your identity once, and its own payout policy says a cash-out may take up to 60 days because every request is reviewed by hand. If that sounds fine, it’s a reasonable app to try. If you want your first dollar today, a $1-minimum app like Scrambly is a better fit.

This review is for US readers only. ZipRewards is limited to permanent residents of the 50 states and DC who are 18 or older.

What ZipRewards is

ZipRewards is a “play games, get paid” app published by Zip Rewards LLC on the US App Store and on Google Play. The idea is simple and common in this category:

  1. You download ZipRewards and sign up. Its site says you can earn your first $2 by answering a few onboarding questions.
  2. You pick a new game from its offer wall, one you’ve never played.
  3. Each game has a ladder of milestones (for example “reach level 20” or “reach board 15”), and each milestone adds a dollar amount to your balance.
  4. Once your available balance hits $25, you can cash out.

Game studios pay apps like ZipRewards to bring them engaged players, and ZipRewards shares part of that with you. That business model is real, and it’s the same one used by Mistplay, Scrambly, Playback and others.

One honest note: the game examples on ZipRewards’ homepage (Monopoly GO, UNO!, Candy Crush and so on, with specific dollar amounts) are marked as illustrative. The site itself says availability changes with advertiser budgets and earnings aren’t guaranteed. Expect the actual games and amounts in your app to differ.

The $25 minimum, explained

The payout policy is unusually clear about cash-outs. You can request $25, $50 or $100 at a time, and only from earnings that are “available.” Earnings still on hold show as Pending and can’t be withdrawn yet.

Other rules from the policy worth knowing before you start:

  • App only. Cash-outs and identity verification happen in the ZipRewards mobile app, not on the website.
  • One request at a time. You can’t submit a new cash-out while another is still pending.
  • One account per person. Multiple accounts, or benefiting from someone else’s, breaks the rules.
  • Rules can change. ZipRewards says it may change the minimum, the amounts and the waiting periods at any time.

$25 is on the high side. Some competitors let you withdraw from $1 to $5, which matters a lot when you’re new and just want to confirm an app actually pays. With ZipRewards you’ll need to finish a meaningful chunk of a game, or a couple of games, before you can test a payout at all.

Identity verification: what to expect

Before your first cash-out, ZipRewards requires a one-time identity check inside the app. According to its payout policy, the flow is:

  1. Complete your profile.
  2. Start verification.
  3. Wait through a 12-hour account review.
  4. Submit the verification form.
  5. After approval, wait six hours before you can cash out.

That’s more friction than some apps, but it’s also standard for apps that pay real money: verification cuts down on fraud and duplicate accounts, which is what lets advertisers keep paying. Do it early so you’re not waiting on it when you reach $25.

How long payouts take

This is the part most “is it legit” articles skip. ZipRewards’ policy says cash-outs are reviewed and paid manually and that payment may take up to 60 days from the date of your request. It lists possible causes of delay: account review, extra verification, support volume, sponsor clawbacks, partner issues, technical incidents and holidays.

“Up to 60 days” is a ceiling, not a promise that it will take that long, and some payouts may be much faster. But you should plan around the worst case. Don’t count on ZipRewards money for a bill.

When the payout is sent, it goes through one of its rewards partners, Tango or Tremendous, to the email address on your account. You choose the reward type when you request it. The policy and site mention options such as Cash App, PayPal, Venmo, Amazon, Visa, Walmart, Starbucks and DoorDash, depending on what the partner can provide at the time. You can see the status of each request, and the reason for any cancellation, in your cash-out history.

Is ZipRewards legit? The evidence

Here’s what points toward “legit”:

  • It’s a registered company (Zip Rewards LLC) with apps on both major US stores.
  • It publishes a long, specific payout policy instead of vague promises, including the uncomfortable parts (manual review, holds, 60-day window).
  • It uses established reward-delivery companies (Tango, Tremendous) rather than an unknown payment system.
  • Rewards are shown in dollars per milestone before you install a game.

And here’s what to keep in mind:

  • Its own blog says it’s legit. ZipRewards publishes an “Is ZipRewards legit?” article on its own site. That’s marketing, not independent proof, so we don’t rely on its claims about ratings or player counts.
  • Independent coverage is thin. When we checked in November 2026, search results were dominated by ZipRewards’ own pages and store listings. There’s not yet a large body of independent reviews or long-running Reddit threads to judge payout reliability over time.
  • Offer tracking can fail. Like every game-rewards app, a milestone only pays if the game’s tracking reports it. Install the game through the app’s link, don’t use a VPN, and screenshot your progress in case you need support.

Our read: there’s no sign it’s a scam, and its policies are more transparent than average. But the $25 floor and manual review mean you’ll invest real time before you find out how it treats you personally.

Who ZipRewards is (and isn’t) for

Good fit if you:

  • Already enjoy casual mobile games and would play them anyway.
  • Don’t mind waiting for a payout.
  • Prefer seeing dollar amounts instead of points.

Not a good fit if you:

  • Need money fast or want to test a payout on day one.
  • Only want to play games you already have installed (these apps pay for new installs).
  • Are uncomfortable with ID verification.

A tip that applies to every app like this: never spend money on in-app purchases to “speed up” a milestone. The math rarely works in your favor, and an offer can fail to track after you’ve paid.

Privacy and permissions

Rewards apps work by tracking which games you install and how far you get, so expect them to collect device and usage information. ZipRewards also needs identity documents for verification. Read the privacy policy and the data-safety section of the store listing before you sign up, and only grant the permissions you’re comfortable with.

The bottom line

ZipRewards appears to be a genuine way to earn some money from mobile games in the US, and it’s more upfront about its rules than many competitors. The trade-offs are real: a $25 minimum, mandatory ID verification, and a policy that allows up to 60 days for payouts. Go in expecting modest, slow rewards for games you’d enjoy anyway, and you’re unlikely to be disappointed.

Frequently asked questions

Is ZipRewards legit or a scam?

ZipRewards is operated by Zip Rewards LLC, publishes a detailed payout policy, and is listed on the US App Store and Google Play. Nothing we found suggests it is a scam, but payouts are manually reviewed and can take up to 60 days under its own policy, so treat it as slow, occasional money.

What is the ZipRewards minimum cash-out?

The payout policy lists cash-out amounts of $25, $50 or $100. You need at least $25 in available (not pending) earnings before you can request a payout.

How long does ZipRewards take to pay?

Cash-outs are reviewed and paid manually, and the policy says payment may take up to 60 days from your request. You also need to pass identity verification first, which includes a 12-hour account review and a 6-hour wait after approval.

Does ZipRewards pay to PayPal, Cash App or Venmo?

Yes. When you cash out you choose a reward type, such as Cash App, PayPal, Venmo, Amazon or Visa. Rewards are delivered by its partners Tango or Tremendous to the email on your account. Available options can change.

Can I use ZipRewards outside the US?

No. The policy requires you to be 18 or older and a permanent resident of the 50 US states or Washington, DC.

Does ZipRewards pay for games I already have?

No. The app asks you to pick a game you've never played from its offer wall. Games already installed on your device generally won't track.
